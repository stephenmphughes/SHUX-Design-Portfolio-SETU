Zenify

A web & mobile platform for youth mental wellbeing built on three pillars: Meditation, Movement, and Music.

Project duration: May ’23 – Sep ’23

Role: UX Designer (end-to-end)

The Product

Zenify is an online and app-based platform aimed at improving young people’s mental health. It blends guided practices and tools across Meditation, Movement, and Music to build daily habits in a youth-friendly, cost-conscious way.

My Responsibilities

User research & interviews, personas, problem statements, competitive audit, IA & wireframes, low/high-fidelity prototypes, usability testing, accessibility, responsive design.

The Problem

Stress, anxiety, and depression are rising among young people. Many lack accessible, structured, and engaging ways to practise wellbeing at home. Zenify set out to deliver a clear, easy-to-use experience that supports daily practice and self-management.

Understanding the Users — Personas

Paul (21)

Problem statement: Paul is a uni student who needs cost-effective meditation to manage exam stress and social anxiety when meeting new people.

  • Profile: Outgoing but under pressure; classes are expensive.
  • Goals: Meet friends; reduce social anxiety.
  • Frustrations: Balancing study/social life; class costs.
Maria (17)

Problem statement: Maria (ASD) wants to interact with music and technology to relax and meditate after school.

  • Profile: iPad apps fan; music therapy helped but costly.
  • Goals: Daily routine; meditative interaction with music.
  • Frustrations: Self-meditation without direction feels repetitive.

Research & Insights

Research combined surveys, interviews, and a competitive audit of meditation and music-therapy apps to understand needs and barriers.

  • Simple navigation and predictable controls across screens.
  • Concise layouts for mobile; clear visual feedback during sessions.
  • Persistent access to primary areas (Meditation, Movement, Music).

Accessibility Considerations

  • Colour-contrast checks throughout.
  • Persistent, predictable navigation (web side menu).
  • Large, tappable targets; clear labels and icon sizing.

Information Architecture

Top-Level
  • Home (Landing / Sign Up CTA)
  • Meditation (Mindfulness, Yoga, Breathing, Affirmations)
  • Movement (Stretching, Running)
  • Music (Player, Artist Profile, Lyric Digest)
  • Tools (Energy Tracker, Sleep Quality, Weekly Schedule, Mood Journal, Targets)
  • Account (Login, Sign Up, Profile, Settings)
Key Elements
  • Media player (audio; planned video)
  • Quick links on Home for faster access
  • Graphs/pie charts for Sleep Quality (planned)

Outcomes & Next Steps

Zenify was developed as a calm digital wellbeing concept spanning both mobile and web. The final outcome demonstrates how the same core experience can adapt across devices while maintaining a consistent visual identity, clear interaction patterns, and a softer wellness-led interface. The next stage would involve expanding the prototypes into a more complete design system, refining microinteractions, and testing the experience with users to validate ease of use, emotional tone, and feature clarity.
