MY DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

What began as a pivot in my career has become a purposeful path in UX and web development, from learning Figma while travelling to completing accredited university qualifications in design and development.

The Beginning.

September 2022

A shift in work life in Spain inspired me to upskill. While travelling, I enrolled in the Google UX Design Professional Certificate, building a foundation in design thinking, research, and prototyping. At the same time, I began learning Figma to practice interface design and create my first prototypes, gaining practical, hands-on skills.

Back to Ireland & 3rd Level Education.

January 2024 - February 2025

Returning to Ireland, I joined DKIT’s UX Design program to gain an accredited qualification, deepen my expertise in user experience, and develop practical skills through hands-on design projects.

Expanding Skills With Graphic Design.

March 2024 - June 2024

While at DKIT, I also completed UCD’s Professional Diploma in Graphic Design. I trained with Adobe Illustrator for vector graphics and logo design, Photoshop for image editing, and InDesign for print-ready layouts.

Advancing in Web Development.

September 2024 - August 2025

I recently completed a Level 8 Higher Diploma in Science in Computing – Web Development at NCI, graduating with First Class Honours. This experience helped me broaden my technical skills and build a solid foundation in web development.

Advancing With Interaction Design.

September 2025 – Present

I'm currently studying a Master’s in Interaction Design at SETU Carlow. The course builds on my UX and web development background, focusing on human–computer interaction, prototyping, and user research. It’s helping me refine how people interact with digital systems — through cleaner, smarter, and more intentional design.
