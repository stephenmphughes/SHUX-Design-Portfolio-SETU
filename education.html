SETU - MA in Interaction Design

Completed August 2026
I completed the MA in Interaction Design at South East Technological University (SETU) Carlow, graduating with Second Class Honours, Grade 2. My major project explored carpooling in Ireland through interaction design, with particular attention to the legal, policy, taxation, safety, trust and reliability issues surrounding shared journeys. The project combined research, survey work, interaction design and prototyping to explore how a digital carpooling service could operate within these wider constraints. It resulted in the Tóggo mobile app concept alongside a separate survey and policy-focused website examining the broader changes that could support such a system. The programme strengthened my ability to look beyond the interface itself and consider how design decisions are shaped by wider systems, while also showing how design research can identify opportunities for improvement across policy, services and digital products.
