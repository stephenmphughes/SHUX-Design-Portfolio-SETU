SETU - MA in Interaction Design

In progress
I am currently studying for a Master’s degree at South East Technological University (SETU) Carlow, where my major project focuses on carpooling in Ireland through the lens of interaction design. The project explores the legal and policy issues surrounding carpooling, alongside key user concerns such as safety, reliability, and trustworthiness. Its aim is to highlight these challenges and investigate how they might be addressed through thoughtful digital design. As part of this work, I am developing a proposed mobile app solution that responds to these issues from both a service and user experience perspective. In parallel, I am also creating a separate case study and survey website that outlines the policy changes needed to support such a system, explains how those changes connect to the mobile app concept, and provides an opportunity for the public to share whether they would use a real-world version of the app if it were developed and facilitated in a business-to-government (B2G) format.
