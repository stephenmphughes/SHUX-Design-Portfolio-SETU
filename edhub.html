edHub

Unified, accessibility-first lesson planning for primary education — AI-assisted and aligned to EAA.

Role: UX research, IA, UI/brand, prototyping

Duration: Concept → MVP roadmap

Overview & Problem

Teachers juggle multiple tools to plan lessons and differentiate for AEN learners. Accessibility is inconsistent, and with the European Accessibility Act, inclusive design becomes baseline. edHub centralises planning, resources, differentiation, and accessibility checks in one responsive workspace.

  • Time drain: scattered tools and rework.
  • Differentiation gap: limited support for learning profiles.
  • Accessibility: need built-in checks and supports (EAA-aligned).

Brand Board and Components

Branding decisions for edHub aim to convey calmness, focus, and inclusivity—qualities vital in ed-tech. The direction balances professionalism with friendliness and accessibility, keeping interfaces clear and engaging.

Colour Palette

Blue is highly favourable in education—its calmness supports focus and creativity. We chose a distinct blue that isn’t overtly saturated to keep the UI modern and unique. A muted yellow accent adds optimism and energy, supporting concentration and visual balance.

#0081A7 • #670643 • #F9D978 • #2D3748 • #FBFAF8

Fonts

Poppins (Bold & Regular) was chosen for a clean, friendly aesthetic with excellent legibility and a polished, contemporary feel. Its geometric forms and generous spacing read clearly at small sizes and scale well across UI components.

Aa Bb Cc — Poppins Bold / Poppins Regular

Bento Grid Design Trend

Bento Grids present multiple elements in a structured, modular layout. They improve scanability, keep the interface organised, and allow related content to be grouped without visual clutter—useful for dashboards and resource hubs.

Glassmorphism, Gradients, Active/ Hover States & Visual Style

Subtle frosted-glass layers and soft shadows create depth and focus without distraction. Used sparingly, this glassmorphism aesthetic helps important elements stand out, encourages interaction, and contributes to a polished, approachable brand identity.

  • Limited support for diverse learning styles - None of the platforms examined offer features for creating lesson plans that cater to AEN students.

  • edHub is designed to fill the gap left by competitors as we consider comprehensive accessibility features, and the ability to customise learning plans for AEN students a key component of our software.
  • Additionally, collaborating with trusted publishers and educational bodies could provide a platform for scaling and gaining credibility quickly.

Design System & Accessibility

  • Tokens: spacing (4/8/12), type scale, semantic colours.
  • Components: cards, forms, tabs, tables (keyboard/ARIA), toasts.
  • Accessibility: AA+ contrast, focus order, skip links, reduced motion, SR labels.

Key Flows

Onboarding, lesson planning, student feedback, subjects navigation, and AI assistance.

1) Onboarding
  • Teachers land into a personalised dashboard after sign-up.
  • Guided steps configure class, subjects, and planning preferences.
2) Plan Lessons
  • AI-seeded templates aligned to Irish curriculum content.
  • Teacher adjusts reading age, tone, and differentiation in-place.
3) Student Feedback
  • Log quick observations and track patterns over time.
  • Export summaries for parent meetings or SEN reviews.
4) Subjects
  • Browse subjects with a simple, consistent layout.
  • Jump into plans, resources, and assessments in a few clicks.
5) AI Assistant – Ask Edy
  • Ask Edy to clarify topics or generate starting points.
  • Teacher reviews and edits before anything reaches students.
